The concept

RiderSafe AI helps international food-delivery riders navigate Melbourne more safely by turning real-time rider hazard reports into multilingual alerts and route updates.

Why it is so simple

Riders on the road have very little attention to spare for a screen. So every screen does one job: reporting is one tap on one of three colours, and alerts can be heard instead of read.

Core features

  • Danger / HazardCrash, dangerous intersection, roadworks. Nearby riders are warned and rerouting is considered.
  • Lane / Path BlockedBlocked bike lane, construction, temporary closure. The spot is marked and an alternative suggested.
  • CongestionHeavy traffic or delivery-zone congestion. Route conditions update and another route is suggested.
  • Multilingual audioShort spoken warnings in the rider's chosen language.
  • Community verification“Still blocked” or “Clear now” keeps reports accurate and removes outdated ones.

How it works

  1. Rider detects a road issue
  2. Taps one quick-report button
  3. Location and time are recorded
  4. Report enters the shared hazard map
  5. Nearby riders receive an alert
  6. Other riders confirm or clear it
  7. A safer alternative route is recommended

Melbourne CBD pilot

The pilot starts in the CBD only. A rider reports a construction-blocked bike lane without help; a second rider receives the alert and decides how to respond.
